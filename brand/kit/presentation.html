What we heard

The brief

A macOS notch app where developers and non-developer entrepreneurs build a team of AI agents on Claude Code or Codex. Each agent has a face, a name and a job; they work, ask before acting, sleep and wake.

friendlycapableorganisedawake

Away from the plain honeycomb and robot-head clichés, readable in the menu bar, works in one colour, reflects a team of agents.

Concept A

Team stack

Three agent cells stacked like the avatars in the collapsed notch — a hive is a team.

  • Mirrors the product: the notch shows your working agents exactly like this
  • Front cell is the one at work; the ones behind are ready
  • Real cut-out gaps, so it works in one colour, reversed and at menu-bar size
